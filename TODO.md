## Bugs

- [x] On the renewal formule step (renewal_form.php), the "Inscription en couple" checkbox and "Conjoint(e)" fields are always shown, even for members with no indication they're part of a couple — should only appear when the member's current subscription/BJ data actually shows a couple status.
- [x] On the renewal "Formule" step (renewal_form.php), when a late renewal falls into the Pack été window, the page still shows the normal subscription radio buttons (Heures Pleines/Heures Creuses, at full price) instead of presenting "Pack été" itself as the offered formula — the Pack été framing only appears later, on the payment/cart step (renewal_cart.php).
- [ ] During Pack été (renewal_form.php Formule step, and the equivalent join summer-pack flow), only "Heures Pleines" should be offered as a formula — currently all three options (Heures Pleines, Heures Creuses, *and* Midi) are shown as selectable, each labeled "(Pack été — tarif unique, voir ci-dessus)". Confirmed via E2E on the renewal flow (throwaway test member, choice=ete path).
- [ ] On the join wizard's Formule step (/inscription), a new member joining during the Pack été window is only ever offered the flat Pack été price for the nearly-finished current season — even when next season's pricing is already published — with no option to join directly for the upcoming season instead. The renewal flow already has this choice (`RenewalService::renewalTarget()`'s `choice_available`); the join flow (ProspectController) appears to have no equivalent. The copy also says "Vous pourrez renouveler normalement à la rentrée," renewal-flavored wording for someone who's never had a membership to renew. Blocks the whole join flow during Pack été, not just this one case — found via the acceptance-test console (scenarios J-01 individuel adulte Garennois Heures Pleines, and J-02 same scenario compétiteur, which hit the identical blocker).
- [ ] In RenewalController::context() (RenewalController.php:461), the pending/approved change-request lookup (pendingChangeRequest) runs with $season->startYear BEFORE the choice-based season advancement at lines 496-498 (the "jump to next season" branch, $season = $season->next()). For a member who picked 'next season' on the Pack été choice screen and then submitted a formula change, the change_requests row is correctly saved under the advanced season — but every later visit to /espace/renouvellement looks up the request under the WRONG (pre-advance) season year and finds nothing. The member never sees 'en attente de validation' or the auto-applied approved change; they just see the formule form again and can resubmit indefinitely. Worse than a display gap: since submit() also gates on context()['redirect'] === null before accepting a new submission, this same lookup bug lets a member submit a SECOND, different renewal choice while a first request is still genuinely pending — confirmed live (a member with a pending couple-status change request successfully submitted a plain individual renewal right after and was sent straight to payment, no block, no error). Fix: move the pendingChangeRequest() call (and the redirect/approved-branch logic that depends on it) to after the choice-based $season reassignment. Found via manual E2E: created throwaway BJ test accounts, picked 'next season', submitted a formula change (Heures Pleines -> Heures Creuses), had the admin approve it, then reloaded the member's /espace/renouvellement — got the plain formule form with the OLD subscription still marked 'abonnement actuel', not the expected change_approved status page.
